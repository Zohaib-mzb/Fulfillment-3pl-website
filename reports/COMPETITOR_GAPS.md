# Competitor gaps: features and how they are presented

Internal research, 2026-11-21. **Structure and features only.** No competitor wording was reused, and no competitor is named anywhere on the site. Sources were public service pages, press releases, app listings and support pages found by web search. Some details are vendor claims or third-party listings, so treat "who offers it" as indicative, not verified.

**Reviewed:** GoBolt, Apple Express, Intelcom, UniUni, Stallion Express, Purolator, ShipHype.

**Scope rule:** we only show services on the approved list (see "Services we offer" in the brief). Features competitors have that are not on that list are marked **Not offered**, and nothing was added for them.

## How the site is now organized

- **Services menu** (in this order): Same day delivery (lead), Next day delivery, Rush and scheduled delivery, B2B and retail delivery, Warehousing and fulfillment, Returns. The footer, homepage cards, "Other services" sidebars and Related blocks all follow the same order.
- **Shown on every service, in one sentence under the homepage cards:** labels and daily manifest, Shopify and CSV (API coming soon), live SMS and email tracking, photo proof with signature on request, EV first fleet, a named account contact, and no minimum volume.
- **Why Kinematics** (6 cards): same day, no minimum volume, one partner, EV first, photo proof, real humans.
