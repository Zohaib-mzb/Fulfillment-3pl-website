# Keyword research and on-page map

Prepared 2026-11-21 for kinematicstech.ca: a new Toronto 3PL and final mile delivery business with no domain history yet.

## How this was researched, and what is missing

- **Google autocomplete (Canada, English)** was fetched for every seed keyword (`suggestqueries.google.com`, `gl=ca`). A suggestion appearing means people search the phrase; no suggestion usually means very low volume. Autocomplete gives **no volume numbers**.
- **Web search (SERP) review** for each cluster showed who ranks today. That is how difficulty was judged:
  - **High:** national carriers, established 3PLs with location pages, directory listicles and review sites dominate.
  - **Medium:** a mix of local providers, vendor blogs and directories.
  - **Low:** few direct competitors, mostly news, research or informational pages, or no exact-match pages.
- **"People also ask" style questions** came from autocomplete question forms and the questions the SERP pages answer. They are reflected in the FAQ blocks and question-style H2s.
- **Not available:** I had no access to Google Keyword Planner, Search Console or a paid SEO tool, so **every volume below is an estimate**, not a measurement:
  - **Very low:** probably under 10 searches a month in Canada.
  - **Low:** about 10–100.
  - **Medium:** about 100–1,000.

  Validate with Keyword Planner and, after launch, Search Console impressions.
- Some autocomplete results were other companies' brand names. Those were excluded and are not named here or on the site.

## Strategy in one paragraph

As a new domain, Kinematics will not outrank established 3PLs or national carriers for head terms like "3PL Toronto" or "same day delivery Toronto" any time soon. Those stay as primary keywords on the pages built for them, because they set relevance. The realistic early wins are:

- **local long tail:** city + service, such as "next day delivery Mississauga" or "same day delivery Brampton"
- **commercial investigation guides:** honest "how to choose the best…" posts, which earn links and rank where listicles are thin
- **niche topics with weak competition:** EV delivery in Toronto, ecommerce returns management

Kinematics never claims to be "best". "Best" queries are answered with neutral checklists.
