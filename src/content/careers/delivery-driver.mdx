## About the role

You are the person our customers' customers actually meet. You will load your van at our sort centre, follow a planned route across the GTA and deliver each parcel with care, photographing every delivery so there is never any doubt about where a parcel was left.

Most routes run in electric vans. Some routes, depending on volume, use a straight truck, which is why we welcome drivers holding a DZ licence as well as G.

## What you'll do

- Load your vehicle in route order and check parcels against your manifest
- Deliver parcels to homes, condos and businesses in Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan and Markham
- Use our driver app for navigation, scanning and photo proof of delivery
- Follow building rules for concierge desks, parcel rooms and lockers
- Collect customer returns on your route when scheduled
- Keep dispatch in the loop about access problems or delays
- Look after your vehicle and report any issues at the start and end of each shift

## What we're looking for

- A valid Ontario **G** licence (a **DZ** licence is an asset for truck routes)
- A clean driving record and comfort driving in city traffic
- Ability to lift and carry parcels throughout a shift
- A friendly, professional manner at the door
- Comfort using a smartphone app all day
- Experience driving an electric vehicle is a plus, not a requirement

## What you can expect from us

- Planned routes, not guesswork
- A dispatch team that answers when you call
- Training on our app, EV driving and charging, and delivery standards
- A team that treats drivers as the professionals they are
