## About the role

Every order that leaves our warehouse represents a brand that trusted us with its customers. As a Warehouse Associate, you'll make sure the right products go into the right box, packed the way the brand wants them, and that every unit on our shelves is where the system says it is.

## What you'll do

- Receive inbound shipments and count them against shipment notices
- Put stock away into its bin location and keep the floor organized
- Pick orders with a scanner and pack them to each brand's standards
- Assemble kits, bundles and subscription boxes
- Inspect and grade customer returns, and restock sellable items
- Support cycle counts and keep inventory accurate
- Hand packed parcels to our sort team for delivery

## What we're looking for

- Attention to detail: you notice when something doesn't match
- Comfort standing, lifting and moving throughout a shift
- Reliability and a good sense of urgency on busy days
- Experience in a warehouse, retail stockroom or fulfillment centre is helpful but not required
- Forklift certification is an asset

## What you can expect from us

- Training on our systems and packing standards
- Clear processes and a tidy, well organized workspace
- A team that values accuracy over rushing
- Opportunities to grow into lead and supervisory roles as we grow
