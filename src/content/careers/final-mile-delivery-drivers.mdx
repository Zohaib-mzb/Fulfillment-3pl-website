## About the role

We are hiring drivers for final mile delivery across the GTA. You will pick up planned routes from our sort centre and deliver parcels to homes and businesses in Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan and Markham, taking a photo at every door.

## What you'll do

- Load your vehicle in route order and check parcels against your manifest
- Deliver parcels safely and on time across your route
- Use our driver app for navigation, scanning and photo proof of delivery
- Keep dispatch updated about access problems or delays

## What you need

- A valid Ontario G or DZ licence
- A clean driving record
- A smartphone
- Comfortable lifting and carrying parcels throughout a shift
