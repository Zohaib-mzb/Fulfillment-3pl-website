## About the role

Dispatch is the nerve centre of final mile delivery. You'll turn the evening's sorted parcels into the next day's routes, make sure each route fits the vehicle driving it, including electric van range, and then keep everything moving once drivers are on the road.

## What you'll do

- Build and balance daily routes using our routing tools
- Assign routes to drivers and vehicles, taking EV range and charging into account
- Monitor routes in real time and help drivers solve problems on the road
- Handle delivery exceptions: access issues, address problems and reattempts
- Communicate with brands and their customers about delivery status
- Spot patterns in exceptions and suggest improvements
- Keep clear, accurate records of each day's operations

## What we're looking for

- Experience in dispatch, logistics coordination or a similar fast paced operations role
- Strong knowledge of GTA geography and traffic patterns
- Calm under pressure and clear on the phone
- Comfort with software tools, spreadsheets and maps
- Excellent written and spoken English; additional languages are an asset

## What you can expect from us

- A central role in a growing operation
- Modern routing and tracking tools
- A team that shares problems early and fixes them together
- Room to shape how dispatch works as we grow
